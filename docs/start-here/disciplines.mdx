# Disciplines and Event Roles

We define work across various **disciplines**. Our disciplines consist of Design, Manufacturing, Electrical, Software, Scouting, Strategy, Media, and Business. These are groups of work that are related to each other and share similar skills. Each discipline is important to the success of the team.

On top of that the team has **Event Roles**. Event roles are specific roles at competition that members are selected to de. Our Event Roles consist of Drive Team, Strategy Specialist, Scouting Specialist, Field Side Media, and the Impact Presentation Team. These roles have specific selection criteria and eligibility requirements.

## Disciplines

| Discipline | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Models the robot in CAD (computer-aided design - software for building 3D models of parts), creates the and integrates the componenets of the robot, and prepares parts for manufacturing |
| Manufacturing | Takes the design and makes them in to real parts, assembles subassemblies, and ensures all parts work together |
| Electrical | Routes wire and cables throughout the robot, installs electical components, and terminates all wires |
| Software | Develops the code for the robot including autonomous paths, robot automation, and driver assists |
| Scouting | Tracks stats of teams we are competing with and against to provide data for the strategy team |
| Strategy | Works on season strategy that drives robot design, develops match strategy for each match at competion |
| Media | Photography and videography of the team, producing content for social media, sponsors, and more |
| Business | Manages buisness plan and interacts with sponsors and outside media |

## Event Roles

### Drive Team

The Drive Team sets up and controls the robot during matches on the field.

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Driver | Controls the robots movement on the field |
| Operator | Controls the robots mechanisms and superstructure on the field |
| Human player | Performs the game specific Human Player tasks, usually introducing game pieces to the field  |
| Drive coach | Coaches the driver and operator to perform optimally, this role is usually filled by a mentor |

More information is available on the [Drive Team page.](/docs/competition/drive-team/criteria)


### Strategy and scouting specialist

The Strategy Specialist and Scouting Specialist are two separate roles, but require working together.

The **Strategy Specialist** helps the Drive Team perform the optimal strategy for a given match. Using scouting data and match observations, they attend the pre match strategy meetings to develop the optimal strategy for the alliance.

The **Scouting Specialist** manages the scouting system. They make sure all scouting systems are functional and that all scouts are capable of collecting the required data.

More information is available on the [Scouting and Strategy Specialist page.](/docs/competition/specialists/criteria)

### Impact presentation team

The **Impact Presentation Team** presents to the FIRST Impact Award judges. They are also required to prepare all materials for the judges. This includes the Essays, presentation materials, script, video, etc.

For more information on the impact award see the [New Member Onboarding page.](/docs/start-here/onboarding#first-impact-award)

For more information on the role, see the [Impact Presentation Team page](/docs/competition/impact/criteria)

### Field Side Media

The **Field Side Media** role records photos and or videos from the field side during our matches. They receive a special badge that allows them to stand near the field during out matches.

More information is available on the [Field Side Media page.](/docs/competition/media/criteria)
