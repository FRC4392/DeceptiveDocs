# About Deceivers Robotics

## About us

Since 2012, Deceivers Robotics (Team 4392) has competed in the *FIRST* Robotics Competition. Each year, students and mentors work in partnership to design, build, and program a robot while marketing the team and its sponsors. That partnership builds technical skills like CAD, programming, manufacturing, and electrical work, along with non-technical ones like leadership, team building, marketing, and project management.

## About FIRST

*FIRST*, which stands for "For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology," is an organization that provides mentor-based robotics programs for students from kindergarten through high school. The *FIRST* Robotics Competition is its high school program, in which teams build a robot each year to compete in a new game. The competition pairs Gracious Professionalism with real-world engineering to inspire students toward careers in science and technology.
