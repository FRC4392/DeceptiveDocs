# Competition

Competition is where the season's work meets the field. The pages in this section cover the jobs every member does at an event — scouting, competition-day procedures, and the pit kit — along with the special roles.

Each special role has two pages. **Criteria** explains how you are selected and is read once before tryouts. The **Manual** explains what you learn and do all season, and is consulted at events.
